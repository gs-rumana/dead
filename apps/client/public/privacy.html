Privacy

What we keep. Playing as a guest stores a random id and a generated name on our server, with your bonds, unlocked classes and lifetime stats. If you sign in with Discord, Google or GitHub we also store that provider's user id and display name, and nothing else from your account.

Anonymous game stats. To balance the game we record runs started and finished, where they end, where players die and what killed them, towns visited, fuel and cash curves, and the frame rate and quality tier per kind of device. These use a random id for each visit and are not linked to your name.

Crash reports. If the game crashes, an error report (the error, browser and device type) may be sent to Sentry so we can fix it.

Never. No ads, no ad trackers, no third-party analytics, no selling data. There is no text or voice chat. Your settings stay in your browser.

To have your account deleted, open an issue on the project's GitHub page with your username.
